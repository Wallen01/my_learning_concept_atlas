# Transformer（轉換器架構）

## 中文速解
以注意力機制為核心、能平行處理序列中各位置關係的模型架構。

## Explanation
Transformer architectures use attention layers to model relationships among tokens. Unlike a purely recurrent design, many computations across positions can be performed in parallel.
