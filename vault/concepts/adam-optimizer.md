# Adam Optimizer（Adam 最佳化器）

## 中文速解
結合動量與自適應學習率、依一階與二階矩估計更新參數的演算法。

## Explanation
Adaptive Moment Estimation (Adam) combines the advantages of Momentum (first moment estimate) and RMSProp (uncentered second moment estimate) along with bias corrections for early steps. It maintains per-parameter adaptive learning rates that make training robust across diverse network architectures.
